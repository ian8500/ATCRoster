Flagship module 01

ATCO Roster

Information-dense monthly planning designed for the reality of operational staffing: watches, dated patterns, requirements, leave, qualifications, changes and controlled publication.

  • Generate with intent — configurable patterns and staffing requirements create a deterministic future baseline.
  • Protect editor decisions — manual overrides are kept separate from generation and preserved through recalculation.
  • Make change visible — joiners, leavers, watch moves and working-arrangement changes create targeted future impacts.
  • Publish with confidence — controlled versions, acknowledgements, reporting and audit provide a clear authoritative roster.

Flagship module 02

Position Monitor

An operational HMI for seeing positions, controllers, roles and configured position-time status at a glance.

  • Understand the current state — open, vacant and occupied positions with primary and secondary controller roles.
  • Show supervision clearly — OJTI and assessor participation remain visibly distinct from the primary controller.
  • Track configured time — position allowances, including time-of-day settings, drive elapsed and remaining indicators.
  • Do not disguise uncertainty — live updates use an explicit stale/degraded warning when current data cannot be confirmed.

Operational Briefing

A clear record that the message reached the right people.

Create controlled operational briefings, direct them to the relevant audience and retain a useful delivery and acknowledgement record.

  • Target deliberately — direct a briefing to the appropriate unit audience instead of relying on unmanaged distribution.
  • Make the current item clear — separate active briefing information from the delivery history around it.
  • Retain delivery evidence — record how a briefing was delivered and retain acknowledgement where required.
  • Support operational continuity — use briefings alongside roster, handover and the live operational picture.
