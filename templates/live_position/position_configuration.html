Cumulative controller-time recoveryAirport-wide policy

Controller time continues across position changes and short breaks. It resets only after the controller has remained out of every primary, OJTI or assessor role for the full recovery period.

Default: 30 minutes out of position. After two accrued hours, each additional commenced hour adds 15 minutes, with escalation capped at four accrued hours.
