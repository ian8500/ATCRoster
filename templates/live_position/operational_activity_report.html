How the figures are calculated

Solo is primary-controller time with no OJTI or assessor logged on. Under instruction and under assessment are primary-controller time while that supporting role is present. OJTI and Assessor are the supporting controller’s own logged-on time. Active sessions are included up to the time the report is run.
